
#================================================================

A service mesh is an infrastructure layer that uses a network of proxies - typically deployed as sidecars alongside application Pods - to manage how services communicate with each other. These proxies handle both ingress and internal (mesh) traffic based on policies defined by a control plane. The control plane distributes configuration, enforces rules, and manages certificates to secure communication.

Envoy(opens in a new tab)
A highly modular and extensible proxy, Envoy is widely favored for its open architecture and commitment to remaining un-monetized. It serves as an effective data plane component in many service meshes and is supported by the community at envoyproxy.io. Its flexibility makes it a popular choice for advanced traffic management.

•
Istio(opens in a new tab)
A comprehensive toolset that leverages Envoy proxies through a multi-component control plane. Designed for platform independence, Istio offers a flexible and feature-rich service mesh, ideal for complex environments requiring robust traffic control and observability.

•
Linkerd(opens in a new tab)
A lightweight, ultrafast service mesh designed for simplicity and ease of deployment. It prioritizes performance and a minimal operational footprint while still offering strong observability and reliability.
